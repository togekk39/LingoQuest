## Current behavior

Chapter 1 and the original 32 A1–A2 lessons run through the offline engine. The textbook uses `mastery` entries keyed by evidence dimension and legacy target. Its four dimensions cover vocabulary recognition, vocabulary recall, grammar understanding, and grammar production. The chapter uses `travelLearning.targets` keyed by concept and skill, with a stage, assistance, and context attached to each authored exercise. The legacy compatibility path can also update a legacy row when a scored chapter item carries a legacy dimension and target. The two views may therefore describe the same submission. Travel reviews can use older matching records to seed a review date with zero new success counters. Keep past aggregates as historical records because they lack the provenance needed to recompute revised results reliably. [Travel architecture](TRAVEL_ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Adaptive learning rules](PHASE_3_ADAPTIVE_LEARNING.md)

Chapter speaking uses `SpeakingPanel` in offline-only mode. A learner can hear the model, rehearse, make and replay a local recording, or advance without the microphone. A reported imitation or recording increments a `speakingPractice` counter. Advancing without a practice report leaves that counter as it was. The chapter writes the practice and advances the checkpoint in the same local operation. Retrying with the old step token cannot count it twice. `Mis habilidades` lists the count under `Práctica oral` and states that speaking ability and pronunciation are still unassessed. The practice has no scored speaking attempts, oral success, or mastery review date. The textbook speaking screen advances the lesson when the learner finishes or skips practice. The optional system recognizer elsewhere can display a transcript, and its provider may require a network connection. The offline assessment route returns `unscorable`. [Offline voice behavior](OFFLINE_EDITION.md)

A chapter response marked `communicationOnly` communicates the intended meaning while leaving the specific target unobserved. The app can offer a model or retry and remember the exposure when evaluating independence or context novelty. The response earns zero target successes or XP and leaves review intervals and pending remediation unchanged.

## Proposed sharing between the chapter and the textbook

1. Author an explicit crosswalk from exercise ID and content revision to a canonical target, skill, observed stage, context, and prompt family. A prompt family groups duplicate renditions of the same task, such as a mission exercise reopened from review. Keep a source field for the chapter or textbook and the original IDs for resuming and showing progress. Validate each mapping against the prompt, allowed help, and what accepted answers actually demonstrate.
2. Record one evidence event for a submission, using the existing unique `clientAttemptId` and the store's serialized write. Save the event, attempt feedback, and affected checkpoint together. The event carries the canonical target, source, context, prompt family, grading revision, aid flags, time, and outcome. One event can carry distinct findings for communication and target form. A correct meaning without the required form can inform feedback while leaving form evidence and its interval untouched. Voice practice, completion, and challenge results keep their separate records.
3. Derive a shared review item from eligible observations for the same canonical target, skill, and stage. A retry or a reopened copy of the same prompt keeps its assistance and exposure history across sources. Same-day repetition of that prompt supplies one scheduling outcome. A separately answered task in a different context can demonstrate breadth. Delayed recall and transfer still require their own time, novelty, and independence conditions. Keep those conditions across courses by reading the same observed practice history. Show the same review item in each relevant course view, with one due date and one reward per qualifying target and day.
4. Preserve the original records in old backups. Only replay a historical submission into the shared evidence view when its stable ID, authored revision, target, time, assistance, and origin can be established. Keep everything else as historical progress or use it to suggest a review that can obtain fresh evidence. Introduce the shared evidence section as optional in format 2 and preserve all existing checkpoints when opening an older file. Keep migration atomic and repeatable by stable event ID. An import follows the existing replacement flow. If progress imports ever merge, retain stable event IDs and resolve overlaps before generating counters.

## Proposed offline speech assessment

| Output                                      | What can support it                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Practice activity                           | A learner reports imitation or makes a recording. Record the activity with its step and practice type.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Spoken content evidence                     | An independent response to a defined task, a usable audio sample, an offline recognizer or a human listener, and a rubric for the intended content and target form. Record the prompt, supports, method, eligible findings, and any abstention separately. A read-aloud task supplies evidence only for the validated read-aloud target. |
| Pronunciation or intelligibility assessment | Calibrated human judgment or a dedicated and independently validated audio-based method. Record the specific construct, evaluator or model version, and the uncertainty. A transcript or recognizer confidence describes the recognizer's output.                                                                                        |

1. On Android, prototype explicitly on-device speech recognition and verify that the installed model and the actual recognition service run in airplane mode. A provider-independent packaged model is another implementation path. Measure device compatibility, bundle size, latency, battery use, and performance with background noise before choosing one. Treat unsupported devices, absent models, denied or revoked permission, and unusable recordings as unavailable. Keep the practice-only workflow available in all cases. [Existing Android voice behavior](OFFLINE_EDITION.md)
2. Start with a small set of authored, meaning-focused prompts. Separate showing a model, hearing a model, a retry, and independent speech. Run audio quality and completion checks before evaluating content. Evaluate intended meaning and target form separately against a reviewed rubric. Abstain when a transcript is ambiguous, truncated, or unreliable and offer a retry or practice. Keep audio and transcripts temporary and local. Persist only minimal validated findings, their provenance, and any support or unavailability status. When a future build exposes content findings to the scheduler, register them under speaking and their actual stage.
3. Compare proposed content judgments with blinded, calibrated human ratings obtained separately for meaning and form. Sample varied accents, device microphones, and quiet and noisy recordings with permission. Report disagreement, false credit for a wrong or unobserved target, unjustified rejection of valid variants, and abstention, including the denominator for each measure. Before exposing automated pronunciation or intelligibility feedback, define its construct, validate agreement with human judgments on held-out speakers and devices, and inspect failure rates across speakers and recording conditions. When those criteria are unmet, keep this part of the workflow at self-review and human assessment.

## Device and learner acceptance

- On actual Android hardware, verify recording, playback, permissions and denial, interrupted sessions, resume, local deletion, and airplane-mode behavior. Verify screen-reader navigation, large text, and keyboard or accessible alternatives. Check that practice and skip leave scored speaking outcomes, review schedules, and mastery unchanged. For a future model, verify the offline guarantee again with the model installed, missing, and outdated.
- Retain the pretest, immediate posttest, and seven-day delayed assessment. Use the same directions and accommodations at each visit, record actual delay and intervening practice, and have a human listener judge the independent oral response. Treat a written accessibility alternative as written evidence. Software tests verify app behavior. Evidence of learning requires results from learners.
