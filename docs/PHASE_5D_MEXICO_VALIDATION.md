## Correction review

The review traces every scored item's vocabulary, meaning, form, grammar and concrete pattern to visible instruction in the available learner paths. The senses of `open` distinguish a place being open from the action of opening. The phrase `start work` uses the employment sense of `work`, while `Does she work?` uses the action. A mention in a dialogue, optional activity or later stage has the instructional scope documented for that stage. The chapter uses existing canonical IDs for equivalent targets and specific IDs where an assessment observes a different sense or pattern. [Mexico prerequisites](../content/source/mexico-prerequisites.ts) · [Teaching evidence](../packages/offline-engine/src/learning-prerequisites.ts)

| Objective     | Prerequisite focus for the correction                                                                                                                                  | Assessment focus                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `routine`     | Teach everyday actions, ordering with `first`, `then` and `after breakfast`, and day periods before using them to decode a sequence or planner.                        | Follow an actual series of actions, interpret a separate schedule, and state a routine for a given purpose.                                               |
| `others`      | Teach subjects and people, specific third-person forms, frequency and time expressions, and the contrast in verb endings.                                              | Report whose routine matters and use its required action, subject and frequency.                                                                          |
