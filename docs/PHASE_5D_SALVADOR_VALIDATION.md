## Team coordination

Rafa’s work stays hidden until a relevant English question confirms the soup. The board displays usual jobs, visible current jobs, confirmed jobs, availability and pending or finished work. When Rafa’s answer confirms the duplicate assignment, Jo finishes washing and becomes available. The player may ask Sam to cut bread and Luz to set out the bowls, or ask Jo to cut bread and Sam to set out the bowls. Alex is resting. Rafa knows how to cut bread and is busy with the soup. The bowls depend on the bread being ready. In the community room, labels wait for a table to be moved and different volunteers can move the table and arrange the labels. [Scenario definitions](../content/source/salvador-scenarios.ts)

A request accepted by a capable and free participant records an assignment. A story advance starts the work. Another story event completes it. Statements and questions evaluate against the actual active assignment. A different participant who receives a duplicate request can explain the existing assignment. A failed, premature or duplicated request keeps the board intact. The confirmation, current assignment, draft and selected speaker are persisted through shared checkpoints and backups. The scenario paths exercise both valid kitchen routes, changed facts, grammatical errors, availability, dependency order and repeated answers. [Language parser](../packages/offline-engine/src/team-language.ts) · [State transitions](../packages/offline-engine/src/team-scenario.ts) · [Scenario cases](../content/source/salvador-scenario-review.ts)

The engine distinguishes `target`, `communication_only`, `context_mismatch`, `incorrect` and `ungradable`. It checks full supported clauses, including polarity and subject agreement. For example, `Jo’s washing the vegetables` demonstrates the affirmative construction and washing only while that task is active. `Can Sam cut the bread?` checks ability. `Sam, can you cut the bread?` requests the task. Open language outside the supported forms gets a clarification or self-review. Story progression and language evidence each use the result appropriate to the attempted activity.

## Review, challenges and offline resources

The repair bank has distinct guides and confirmations for be omission or agreement, spelling, habits, states, question order, negation, can + base form, can/can’t listening, current availability, work status and sequence. After a guide the error remains pending until an independent response in a different situation. Archived text repair includes the scenario facts in its prompt. Separate text repairs preserve the form or construction they assess when the pack is removed. Listening contexts require their installed clips. Same-day transfer and delayed retention use separate rules. Each challenge mode adds one item per approved objective, with reviewed requirements and a matching independent practice path.
