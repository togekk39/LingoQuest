## Revision 17 final corrections

The first regression tests reproduced three problems. She selected the addressee as its referent, an ability inquiry could satisfy a work check, and Can you was treated as a request in an ability turn. The evaluator distinguishes the player, addressee, named third parties and defined groups. He and she require an explicit antecedent from the clause or dialogue. We and they require group facts. Habit and be agreement follow grammatical person. Contractions, natural time positions and complete polite variants are supported. Ambiguous and incomplete claims receive clarification.

Ability, availability, current work, ownership, completion and agreement have distinct records. Each turn declares the fact it needs. Can you can ask about capability or request work according to that turn. An offer receives a suggestion based on outstanding work. Assignment requires acceptance, capability, availability and task prerequisites. A communicative alternative can help the exchange. Independent evidence follows the declared assessment and demonstrated form.

The first vegetable batch and rice are complete in the shared history. The second mission reads a morning snapshot. The fourth imports earlier work and introduces a separately identified final batch. The player delivers a live handoff while bread is being cut and bowls are still waiting. A later procedure is for another team. In the display, moving the table and putting out labels are the required coordination work. Bia and Leo continue their background work after the coordination milestone. Story and Study use the same progress and backups carry the scenario and its draft. The first completed live record remains available for handoff when a mission is replayed.

The evaluator separates positive and negative statements, information and yes/no questions, affirmative and negative ability, ability inquiries, requests, availability, instructions and handoffs. Missing be in What Jo doing? selects a question repair. The same omission in a statement selects affirmative progressive repair. Cutting receives independent confirmation with a different person, place and object. A guide leaves the original error pending until independent confirmation. The optional next-lunch note has four separate responses and evidence for the past, habit, current action and request.

Revision 16 upgrades by stable step and assignment identity. The fourth mission's wash becomes the final batch. Owner, status, selected teammate and draft are retained. An already answered confirmation or handoff can be repeated with recorded support when its requirement changed. Historical receipts and evidence keep their original revision.
