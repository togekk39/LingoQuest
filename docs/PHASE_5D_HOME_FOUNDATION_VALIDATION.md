# Phase 5D-2A Home Foundation Validation

[Español (Latinoamérica)](PHASE_5D_HOME_FOUNDATION_VALIDATION.es-419.md)

Home serves Latin American Spanish speakers who are starting English. This review follows the ten approved Home objectives and the existing plaza, bus stop and lost-property story. The starting point is `f41a18b`, the merge of Phase 5D-1 through PR #31. The baseline uses Home chapter pack revision 7 and Mexico City revision 9 in `catalog-v4.json`. ## Entry points and pace

Home continues to use the three main missions and the existing plaza, bus stop and lost-property scenes. In the updated pack, Luz opens name-card practice as the learner meets Sam and offers the travel registration materials later. At the bus stop, Sam offers short instruction, clarification, location and route activities. Luz introduces people, objects and ownership at lost property. Sam offers a final combined activity after the bag is returned. A returning learner can also open the foundation lessons from **Aprender → Inglés desde cero**. Story and Study open the same mission definitions. [Home adventure](../packages/offline-engine/src/chapter-story.ts) · [Home lesson authoring](../content/source/home-foundation.ts)

The learner can take a break during an activity and return to the saved step. The foundation encounters are optional and the original route diagnostic still recommends where to begin. Sam and Luz keep their own identities. Introduction and spelling practice use the learner’s chosen name, including a practice name. [Home adventure](../packages/offline-engine/src/chapter-story.ts) · [Answer grading](../packages/contracts/src/answer-grading.ts)

Each NPC recommendation opens one existing activity. The selector suppresses completed or sufficiently evidenced activities, prefers a paused draft, and can suppress the location recommendation after a relevant unaided diagnostic result. Diagnostic evidence leaves formal teaching and mastery separate. The main route requires reviewed prerequisites before a graded response. Missing references produce a short teaching prompt. Extra letter checks, broader number contexts, recording and the library transfer remain available in Story or Study. Aprender recommends a paused mission, needed practice, or review without changing the player's location or passport.

## Audio and supported practice

The lessons pair each English model with short Latin American Spanish explanations. Activity audio is packaged for replay. The Home models follow US letter names, including `zee` for Z. The regional `zed` model is also available. The local Flite American English voice uses explicit pronunciations for letter names and the intended forms of `Read`, `Close` and `Excuse me`. Pronunciation practice uses listen-and-compare recordings and explains mouth position, word stress and final sounds in Spanish. [Audio authoring](TRAVEL_ASSETS.md) · [Chapter media builder](../scripts/chapter-media.ts)

Sam and Luz can respond to requests for a repeat, a slower delivery or the meaning of a line at designated dialogue turns. The learner hears the request as a full expression, followed by the ordinary or slowed NPC audio when appropriate. The meaning response shows the NPC’s gloss. The repair lesson teaches when to choose the fixed expressions and provides separately keyed exercises. [Dialogue practice](../apps/mobile/src/travel/StoryAdventure.tsx) · [Repair instruction](../content/source/home-foundation-literacy.ts)

Seeing a translation, following a model, imitation and recording supply supported practice evidence. Home’s open speech steps request self-check and local recording through their activity settings. An audio choice can support independent listening when the required teaching is present and the learner has heard the audio, subject to the recorded assistance. Pronunciation and spontaneous oral interaction need an observer to assess those abilities. [Speaking settings](../apps/mobile/src/travel/TravelFlow.tsx) · [Unified learning evidence](PHASE_5C_LEARNING_EVIDENCE.md)
