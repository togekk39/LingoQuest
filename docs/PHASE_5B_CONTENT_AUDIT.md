# Phase 5B · Curriculum quality audit

[Español (Latinoamérica)](PHASE_5B_CONTENT_AUDIT.es-419.md)

This review starts from Phase 5A’s merge at `78df72f`. It compares LQP 2.1.0 and the published chapters with LQP 2.2.1 and the active chapter revisions. The editorial pass covered objectives, en-US English, es-419 Spanish, keys, distractors, feedback, teaching sequence and audio source text. Independent human validation remains open for the items at the end of this report.

The [item ledger](PHASE_5B_CONTENT_AUDIT.items.tsv) identifies all 839 tracked entries. `retained` marks an unchanged record, `revised` a changed record and `added` a new ID. The ledger includes the full before-and-after record for edits and the new record for additions. Fingerprints identify the immutable payloads and let `npm run curriculum:verify` verify the ledger. A chapter ID and card position identify each travel question. To regenerate it after rebuilding the course and chapters, run `npm run curriculum:verify -- --write-audit-index`.

## Verification and remaining evidence

`npm run curriculum:verify` checks the bilingual master plan, package/source parity, stable IDs and 1,567 grading probes for 570 keyed entries. It checks 93 entry paths for the course, reader and Story, 32 Story/Study prefixes used for adaptive review, and 48 optional challenge rank-and-mode combinations. The assessment records cover 124 course challenges, 15 Home challenges and 18 diagnostic items. The 20 cards name 67 exact pattern forms, alongside required vocabulary and grammar. The checker uses the authored sense and form references, current teaching stages and actual completion gates. A changed prompt, key, source or formal lesson requires editorial review. Negative cases include a grammatical exercise with an untaught word, a form that appears only in the preview, a changed sense with the same spelling, a skipped teaching stage, and a challenge stamped by diagnostic skips.

Home, Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Brazil, Chile, Miami, New York and South Africa use revision 7. The other eleven chapters remain on revision 6. LQP 2.2.1 is the identical Web bundle and source for the course units and activities in Android’s packs. `catalog-v2.json` points new apps to these bytes. The earlier Android 2.1.0 catalog and immutable files are preserved. A failed background refresh leaves installed materials usable. The state format and completed exercise, lesson, mission, passport and journal IDs are retained. When a new practice item is appended to an unfinished lesson that had reached speaking, the learner resumes at the first pending item.

Human follow-up remains open for regional Spanish register, actual CEFR difficulty and cut scores, learner retention and transfer, and full audio playback, stress and intelligibility on physical Android devices. The Toronto synthesized letter sequence was checked, including American `zee`. Static media checks verify each required phrase, supported media type and artifact checksum. The Chapter 1 audio in the browser and the new Salvador clip have dedicated playback tests. Human and learner evidence is needed for pronunciation and spontaneous communication. The course keeps `draft_needs_human_review` until this evidence is available.
