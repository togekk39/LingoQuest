## Route and cultural design

Coordinates are approximate city reference points in longitude, latitude order. Local languages and practices shape the situations. The course teaches American English with es-419 instructions, and characters turn to English when they need a shared language. Local expressions can appear with a gloss.

Use a new host, event or invitation to explain longer flights and distinguish each region by how people get around and use public spaces.

## IDs, saved state and offline upgrades

- Source labels, map positions, covers and card copy change under the stable IDs in the table. Existing lesson, objective, exercise, canonical learning and pack IDs remain addressable. The master plan retains every prerequisite edge. Shared language targets use the existing canonical IDs.
- A historical card record keeps its original material ID, material version and context. New card events keep the same material and exercise family with revision `19` and a `.route-19` context suffix. The card question remains guided and the production model remains a self-check. These events supply evidence only within their recorded scope.
- Salvador's item remains `salvador-valparaiso-postcard`. The saved `salvador-postcard-read` event and quest reward resolve to **Postal para Lisboa** and chapter `chile`. Revision 19 updates text and keeps the underlying mission, scenario and checkpoint revision 18. When an older Salvador pack is installed, the loader projects the new postcard text from the same story IDs. Saved inventory, team work, evidence, archived review and stamps retain their existing identifiers. Brazil 18 evidence can still qualify for Brazil 19 challenges because the audited assessments, prerequisites and scenario rules match.
- First-time downloads use catalog v14. Home through Cusco retain their existing packs and checkpoints. An ongoing Salvador run continues from its current step. Completed Salvador players can follow **Continuar a Lisboa** from the passport. Existing users of the four old cards can practice offline under the archive label and request the new card when connected. A failed request leaves the installed card usable. Format 2 backups can restore chapter progress, the souvenir and evidence with the same IDs.
