The 24 units and 83 lessons are sufficient as an **initial core outline**. An end-to-end B2 program still requires implemented materials and measured practice. Keep the 20 stops and lesson IDs. First add longer input, output with feedback, additional contexts and delayed parallel tasks inside the existing units. Add core lessons only if trial evidence reveals a persistent prerequisite or progression gap. The [master plan](curriculum-master-plan.md) specifies each lesson. The [vocabulary plan](vocabulary-coverage-plan.md) gives text, speech and workload ranges.

## Planned objectives, materials and assessment

The lesson references below are planned. Each assessment uses an unseen parallel task with the conditions in the [authoring standard](lesson-authoring-standard.md). **Gap** names material to commission before treating the objective as covered. **Pilot** identifies a judgment requiring a reviewer, interlocutor or learner evidence. Listening and reading checks use keyed critical details or a sourced answer grid. Speaking and writing use separate rubric ratings.

**Language progression.** Pre-A1 uses receptive print/sound and formulaic chunks. A1 adds word order, subject agreement, do-support, basic tense and quantity. A2 adds comparisons, plans, experience vs finished time, requirements and repair. B1 moves to time/aspect choices in connected accounts, conditionals, relative clauses, reported instructions and basic stance. B2 extends those tools to qualification, concessions, passive and reporting choices, hedging, source attribution, counterfactuals and audience-directed cohesion. Each construct is taught before it becomes required in an assessment. [Skill Dependency Map](skill-dependency-map.md) · [Vocabulary & Language Coverage Plan](vocabulary-coverage-plan.md)

## Diagnostic depth and transition checks

- **Pre-A1 → A1.** Move from matching and repeating to retrieving details in a new identity form and asking for clarification. Preserve audio, print and speaking results separately.
- **A1 → A2.** Reuse A1 quantities, schedules and questions in parallel planning tasks in Valparaíso and service tasks in Toronto. Add one changed constraint and require confirmation and a usable note without a visible model.
- **A2 → B1.** In `lq-london-disruptions.notices` and `lq-rome-problems.background`, start with a short familiar input. Then add a connected narrative, time shift, second document or speaker and an unresolved detail. Require a connected account, a clarification and a response to that clarification.
- **B1 → B2.** In Hobart and Wellington first compare two short, mostly explicit positions. Next lengthen sources and add conflicting assumptions and qualified evidence. In Cape Town and the capstone require a supported judgment about source credibility, spontaneous follow-ups, an acknowledged counterargument and an adapted report. A routine factual exchange or longer text with only literal-detail questions supplies B1-like evidence even if its chapter carries a B2 label.
- **Evidence before a level claim.** Use two parallel forms with different prompts and contexts. Give one at least seven days after initial success. Keep the target, support conditions and difficulty demands comparable and include a non-travel context. Separate correct practice, immediate unaided performance, retention, transfer and unobserved skills. Report each modality and any accommodations. For B1/B2 use trained human marking until an alternative is validated, double-rate a sample, compare agreement and inspect subgroup and genre gaps. Local thresholds guide feedback. Set band cut scores only after pilots and a documented standard-setting process.
