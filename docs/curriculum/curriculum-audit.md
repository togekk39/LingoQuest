## Textbook inventory and reuse, all 32 lessons

Source and bundled counts match. There are 200 new vocabulary IDs, 68 review appearances, 32 primary grammar IDs, 12 original placement items, 40 pronunciation drills and two original standalone missions. The missions, placement and drills travel with the whole-course payload and are distinct from the Home RPG. Textbook prerequisites are generated as a chain to the immediately preceding grammar ID. That proves order, with limited information about conceptual dependencies such as output by modality. Lesson completion requires visiting every exercise; it can happen after all incorrect answers. References and key shape pass structural verification. `validateReferences` checks cumulative token availability in exercise materials with gloss support. It does not infer grammatical demand, inspect everything embedded in Spanish directions, or establish what was rehearsed independently. ## First chapter, review, placement and challenge behavior

- Home ships four RPG encounters in the plaza, bus-stop and lost-property scenes. Branching through the bench or Luz leads to the bag and postcard. The three teaching missions `hello` (9 steps), `bus` (8) and `bag` (8) contain nine focal vocabulary cards, 14 scored exercises and two optional speaking activities. `hello` ranges from greetings to typed introductions and a photo-signup transfer. `bus` moves from a ticket/location question to listening, guided dictation, word order and a map change. `bag` mixes color and ownership reading, a typed description and a new stall context. Retain the accessible teaching and two branches. Review overlap and scene prerequisites before enabling other starting points. In `bag_teach` the color and article examples are taught in context. The earlier `bus` dictation accepts `It is` for the heard `It's` as a supported form variant. A future independent listening key should distinguish exact transcription from meaning. [Story](../../packages/offline-engine/src/chapter-story.ts) · [Teaching missions](../../packages/offline-engine/src/travel-content.ts) · [Prior item audit](../CHAPTER_1_CONTENT_AUDIT.md)
- The six Home placement items recommend `hello`, `bus` or `bag`. A transcript-aided audio placement response is excluded from its score. The 16 discrete review/remediation exercises feed travel practice. The travel scheduler records assistance, exposure by exercise/context, and independent delayed success using 1/3/7/14/30-day intervals. Transfer credit checks context novelty; a separate textbook scheduler has four dimensions and a 0.65 ceiling before delayed/varied evidence conditions. Imitation and recording can add oral practice; the offline speech assessment route returns unscorable. Preserve the practice and scheduled remediation. Resolve cross-entry equivalence and listening provenance before combining records. [Placement](../../packages/offline-engine/src/travel-placement.ts) · [Travel learning](../../packages/offline-engine/src/travel-learning.ts) · [Evidence notes](../LEARNING_EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION.md)
- Home trials display 13 ranks but only ranks 1–3 can start, after the chapter gate. Home has five prompts per type. The original textbook pack supplies 13 rank configurations and 124 items (70 grammar, 30 cloze, 24 reading), with content predominantly routine present, time and frequency. The shipped pool has no `challenge_travel_` IDs. The separately authored 57-item expansion is not imported by `build-course-pack.ts`; the engine's higher-tier travel branches are dormant with the shipped pool. Rank difficulty also changes clocks and hearts. Local practice affects challenge records without proficiency credit. Keep it optional and give untimed parallel practice.
