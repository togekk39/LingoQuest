## Entry, evidence and continuity

1. From Story, a player clicks an NPC, previews or refreshes a taught skill, and uses it for a scene outcome. Offer a supportive way to proceed if the learner is exploring or unable to produce a particular modality. Close and resume from the saved NPC/turn, or open the linked Study task. From Study, load the same canonical lesson and supplies without replaying conversations or earning a stamp. Keep the journey low pressure and rewards cosmetic.
2. Author a task reference to the canonical lesson and exercise. Only add a new variant where the story changes the prompt or context. Label source entry, prompt family, context, mode, stage, help and content revision. Log a single idempotent attempt event. Update matching targets and the review schedule from that event. Persist source-specific checkpoint and story outcomes separately. If a story option and a Study prompt differ in evidence, both point to the same concept and have different eligible targets or stages. The shared queue can open a safe independent variant from either entry.
3. Preserve `travel_home`, `hello`, `bus`, `bag`, `u01_l01` through `u08_l04`, exercise IDs and durable story facts. The new `lq-` IDs describe the canonical roadmap. When splitting or moving published content, keep old IDs addressable for history and compatibility, and add reviewed mappings at target level. For example, map written introduction meaning to `lq-home-start.name:w`, and map `I am` specifically only when observed. `travel_bus_direction` maps to guided understanding of the location phrase. `u08_l02` directions serve as earlier Home teaching and later differently scoped transfer. Preserve an old completion as historical and invite a short placement or review when evidence is unknown.
4. Extend the existing chapter manifest from an optional single `unit` and Home-only `story` to versioned references for multiple units and optional encounters per stop. Make the same canonical registry available to local Android and Web readers. Use `content/source` for shared authorship, `packages/course-pack` for strict compilation/validation and `packages/offline-engine` for local delivery and review. Do not force current artifacts into a widened schema that old readers fail to parse. Negotiate capabilities with an app/schema version and preserve old catalog and artifact paths. [Current pack schema](../../packages/course-pack/src/index.ts)
5. Choose separate resource lifetimes for compact lesson/review data, recordings and heavy scene art. First launch can download a compact starter pack; after setup the learner can revisit retained units and scheduled tasks without connectivity. Make previews show size, offline availability and removal consequences. Install into a staged transaction, verify checksum, version, dependencies, media and space, and activate only on success. Leave old resources active after an interrupted update. If learners remove a chapter, keep progress and show which tasks require an explicit redownload. Avoid marking inaccessible review tasks failed; offer a matching installed variant or postpone. Cover installation, return to Home, airplane mode and restart on both platforms. [Current download and storage](../../apps/mobile/src/lib/chapterPacks.ts)

Keep only branch choices that change a conversation, clue, arrangement or outcome. Reuse current conventions for postcards and checkpointed encounters. Give fictional or age-neutral alternatives for scenarios involving jobs, family, health or personal details. The player can choose a name and always remains a separate character.
