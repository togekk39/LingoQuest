# Phase 5D Chapter Standard

[Español (Latinoamérica)](PHASE_5D_CHAPTER_STANDARD.es-419.md)

Use this standard for each new or revised Phase 5D adventure. Keep the destination, course unit and Can-do IDs from the master plan. Use the existing offline engine and its learning ledger for Story, Study, review and challenges. A chapter review records instructional coverage, actual results and open acceptance work. [Master plan](curriculum/curriculum-master-plan.md) · [Lesson authoring](curriculum/lesson-authoring-standard.md) · [Learning architecture](PHASE_5C_LEARNING_ARCHITECTURE.md)

A chapter that starts from zero should offer one evidence-based activity in context. Diagnostic answers can guide order. Require visible instruction for the exact knowledge a graded task needs. For generated assessment banks record the target, exact requirements, distractors and support for every generated item. Keep record by ability and individual letter or number.

## Chapter data and instruction

| Record                         | Required material                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Chapter and mission            | Stable chapter, scene, quest, step, dialogue and item IDs. Define the actual route through NPC turns, object interactions, explanations, practice, assessment and the saved chapter outcome. Bind each assessed step to the relevant lesson and canonical target IDs.                                                      |
| Reviewed language dependencies | List the exact vocabulary sense, lexical form, grammar rule and pattern required to understand the task and produce an acceptable answer. | Formal teaching                | Name the visible card, example, dialogue turn or explanation for each dependency. Teach the word with its intended meaning and form in context. | Assessment                     | Record the Can-do objective, material and item IDs, context, assessed dimension, reference or media used, and accepted variants or self-check criteria. Identify permitted help and the evidence it yields. For every keyed prompt, give a valid answer and plausible errors with feedback.                                |
| Review and challenges          | Link reviewed teaching to a new instance of the same assessed target, a specific remediation activity, delayed practice or an optional challenge. Keep the language and evidence eligibility tied to the relevant skill and mode.                                                                                          |
| Publication                    | Record the content revision, authored definitions, assets, compatibility catalog, checksum, bytes and verification results. Trace all available Story and Study paths from their real entry points. A returning player, a learner who opened Study first, and a learner who missed an optional scene all need an eligible lesson route. Use reviewed prerequisite records for every scored item. Record a formal teaching receipt only after the learner reaches the visible instruction that supports the exact reviewed reference. Treat previews, earlier contextual mentions, translations, optional dialogue and visible models according to their actual instructional and assessment role. Changing a reviewed explanation or vocabulary sense requires renewed review of the affected receipts and assessments. Teaching later in the chapter leaves earlier attempts without qualifying preparation. For a chapter that starts from zero, introduce print, sound and meaning together in short es-419 explanations with English audio. Teach fixed expressions as complete units before expecting the learner to analyze their parts. Give a directly selectable module all the prior teaching its checks need on that route. Use the same module and item IDs at its Story and Study entrances. Keep optional modules reachable after the main quest ends and preserve the player's place when they pause. ## Assessment and evidence

Use natural English and clear es-419 instructions. For controlled answers, combine the form target with the prompt's necessary facts, such as who, when, where and which event. Enumerate or parse supported alternatives within those limits and verify contractions, spelling, date and time formats and equivalent word order where appropriate. Test fluent answers with the wrong person or time along with malformed versions of a correct fact. An independent item must use the actual audio, reading or scene it claims to assess. Explain an error in terms of the learner's answer and give a practical next attempt. [Answer grading](../packages/contracts/src/answer-grading.ts) · [Evidence rules](PHASE_5C_LEARNING_EVIDENCE.md)

When a service exchange offers several valid combinations, the item records both the English form and the facts that make the response actionable. Store which order the player placed and derive or select the corresponding follow-up. Include incorrect quantities, items and restrictions in the keyed checks. Treat a natural expression using a different form as communication evidence when it fits the situation. Teach different senses and units explicitly when they change what a quantity means. Use the local currency and teach its spoken amounts before independent price tasks. [Cartagena validation](PHASE_5D_CARTAGENA_VALIDATION.md)

An open writing or speaking task has an audience, purpose, and observable self-check criteria. Reveal optional models after the learner has an opportunity to respond. Record self-review, permitted hints, translations, transcripts, models and retries as appropriate support or practice. A recording or a recognized transcript can help the learner compare spoken content. Pronunciation and spontaneous interaction require an assessment that observes those abilities. Store the activity and the permitted evidence fields. Use the platform's normal text fields so an eligible answer submits with Enter, and preserve composition and deliberate multiline entry. [Keyboard tests](../tests/e2e/keyboard.spec.ts)

Author playback and self-check capabilities with the activity. Use an installed clip when English letter names, spoken codes, or specific sound contrasts are part of the task. Dialogue can offer a repeat, slower delivery or a meaning gloss while the current turn remains active. Carry the support to any answer it helps. A learner-selected name can anchor a spelling check. Keep its accepted punctuation and name forms independent of the NPCs' names. [Home item audit](PHASE_5D_HOME_FOUNDATION_VALIDATION.items.tsv)

Reuse canonical IDs across Story, Study, review and challenges. A shared target can receive different evidence dimensions from separate prompts. Preserve item identity, revision, context, support, outcome and time. Record independent success only when the required teaching is established and the task conditions support it. Transfer checks use an independently answered prompt in a different context. Record retention only after the required delay and a distinct prompt. A same-day transfer may demonstrate application while retention remains pending. [Learning evidence](PHASE_5C_LEARNING_EVIDENCE.md) · [Evidence reducer](../packages/offline-engine/src/learning-evidence.ts)

## Stateful exchanges

Define a versioned scenario with the facts each step can rely on. In a meal these are the menu, prices, participants and their requirements. Give each selectable item a stable ID and identify the English forms allowed for items, quantities, units, people and changes. Attach the permitted language action to the assessed turn. The verb and arguments must be understood together. A request that mentions an item in a negative statement has different behavior from a request to add that item.

Evaluate the English form and the current scenario before applying an action. Valid target and communication-only responses may advance or update the scenario. A mismatch in quantity, person, menu, replacement or dietary constraint leaves the saved state intact and gives specific feedback. Use a clarification or self-check when the offline rules cannot establish the intended action. Render the NPC reply, confirmation and totals from the committed facts. Keep author-facing answer examples out of the independent prompt. A menu calculator can help compare options while a submitted English response controls the actual conversation.

Map a graded response to the exact construction and skill it demonstrates. Keep grammar, vocabulary senses, word forms and prerequisites distinct. A target-format assessment awards independent evidence only for the applicable taught forms when the conditions for independence are met. A natural alternative can advance the story and record the narrowly scoped language it demonstrates. Keep remediation tied to the same construction and ability in a different context. A related construction can have its own evidence while the original error remains pending.

Save scenario facts, amendments, pending confirmation and the answer draft with the shared Story and Study checkpoint. Reopen the same state after a restart, a change of entry point or a backup restore. Guard writes against duplicate submissions and give a fresh run fresh state. Preserve historical evidence and back up values needed to resume the exchange. Archive the small amount of authored data needed to review a situation offline. Keep source media in the downloadable pack. Verify two complete paths with different selections, a real replacement, an invalid answer and the resulting totals through normal entry points. [Cartagena validation](PHASE_5D_CARTAGENA_VALIDATION.md)

For event reconstruction, keep the authored date, time, place, action and people immutable. Declare what each witness knows and which facts are visible, missing or disputed. A question can verify a fact or receive a clarification while the historical event stays fixed. Save the player’s corrected note after checking context and language. Let more than one inquiry order reach the correction and preserve the selected witness and the draft on pause. [Cusco](PHASE_5D_CUSCO_VALIDATION.md)

## Remediation, progression and offline continuity

When a known error occurs, guide the learner through a concrete contrast or model. After a successful guided attempt, offer a different confirmation prompt that assesses the same target and ability. Keep the correction pending until the independent response meets the criteria. Preserve defaults for safe exploration, untimed learning, optional help and progression through the story. Let the learner switch entry points and resume the saved stage, answer or result. [Adaptive review](PHASE_5C_ADAPTIVE_REVIEW.md)

Keep challenges optional. Gate every scored challenge item on its exact formal teaching and relevant prior independent normal practice. For a rank, check whether the available qualified items fulfill the mode's existing run rules. Exercise ordinary progression and earlier challenge histories in the tests. [Challenge gate](../packages/offline-engine/src/challenge-learning.ts)

Publish the next immutable chapter revision with the catalog used by compatible clients. Keep earlier catalog revisions usable by their clients. Include the chapter's references, reviewed paths, graded keys, review material and required offline assets. Keep eligible lightweight text review available after pack removal through the review archive. Preserve prior schedules, answers and progress. For a task that needs a missing media file, retain the review state and offer the available text practice or the appropriate way to restore access to the item. [Chapter packaging](CHAPTER_PACKS.md) · [Learning persistence](../packages/offline-engine/src/learning-persistence.ts)

When extending an existing adventure, keep historical receipts tied to the exact stages and revisions they identify. Give new material a separately reviewed scope and keep required story milestones stable when adding optional practice. Exercise the upgrade using an installed prior pack and real progress through the previous revision. [Home foundation validation](PHASE_5D_HOME_FOUNDATION_VALIDATION.md)

## Chapter acceptance checks

| Check                                | Required evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instruction before assessment        | Audit each item's exact dependencies against its earlier visible teaching. Verify rejection or supported handling with missing vocabulary, a different sense, a missing form, edited instruction, later instruction and an alternate entry path.                                                      |
| Grading and independence             | Exercise natural variants and relevant incorrect facts, including the wrong person, time, day and place where assessed. Verify that guided practice, a visible answer, a transcript and retries produce their corresponding evidence.                                                                 |
| Shared progress and delayed evidence | Verify Story-to-Study and Study-to-Story mapping, separate skill dimensions, a same-day different-context success and a delayed independent response. Exercise starting from saved state and an older backup.                                                                                         |
| Review and unlocking                 | Exercise a specific wrong answer, guided repair, a different independent confirmation and a pending correction when an eligible variant is unavailable. Verify challenge denial when teaching or relevant independent evidence is missing.                                                            |
| Pack and persistence                 | Verify checksums and authored content parity, installed media, failed transfer handling and compatibility catalogs. Cover a fully offline chapter route, a restart during an unfinished answer, access to earlier progress and lightweight review after removal of the new pack.                      |
| Builds and release records           | Run the curriculum verifier, course and chapter pack verifiers, compile and lint checks, unit tests, Playwright suite, Web and Android exports and the signed APK build. Record the tested revision, verified APK signature and checksum, and the actual GitHub Actions result or its current status. |
| Human and device review              | Review every bilingual cue, prompt, rubric, key and media link. Check interaction targets at phone size and verify language, context, device audio and airplane mode with the resulting installed chapter. Use educator and learner review to support teaching and CEFR claims.                       |

Document item-level findings with the exact item ID, assessed target, prior teaching, at least one acceptable and one relevant incorrect example, grading or practice classification, and a reproducible test or review reference. Group findings by release milestone and keep the acceptance record current. [Mexico City validation example](PHASE_5D_MEXICO_VALIDATION.md)
